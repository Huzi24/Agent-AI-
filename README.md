## 🎯 About the Project

**MediAgent AI** is engineered for the **PayPal AI Hackathon** to revolutionize digital health commerce through **Agentic AI**. Millions of patients struggle daily with deciphering complex handwritten prescriptions, navigating volatile medicine pricing across fragmented pharmacy networks, and managing recurring refills[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span)[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span)[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span)[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span)[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span). 

MediAgent AI acts as an autonomous medical procurement concierge: it ingests prescriptions via advanced computer vision, evaluates real-time pharmacy inventories and pricing matrices using **AG Grid**, splits payments between insurance copays, and securely executes transactions using the **PayPal Developer Platform (Sandbox)**[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span)[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span)[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span)[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span)[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span).

---

## ✨ Key Features & Architecture

* **AI Prescription Vision Parser:** Instantly extracts medication names, active ingredients, exact dosages, and refill frequencies from uploaded medical scripts or doctor's notes.
* **AG Grid Pharmacy Arbitrage Matrix:** Interactive, high-performance data grid comparing local and online pharmacy stock, generic vs. branded alternatives, and real-time pricing indices.
* **Autonomous Agentic Checkout (PayPal):** Automatically builds optimized medical carts and executes secure payments via PayPal APIs with human-in-the-loop authorization.
* **Smart Copay & Refill Automation:** Automatically calculates insurance coverage splits and schedules automated 30-day recurring refills.
